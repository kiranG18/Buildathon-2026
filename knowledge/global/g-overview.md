<!-- K-001 -->
Helix Agents is an AI agent platform for enterprise workflows. Agents automate approvals, onboarding, data sync and voice tasks under central governance.

<!-- K-002 -->
Deployment options: managed cloud in US, EU and India regions, or inside the customer VPC. Role-based access and full audit logs ship in every plan.
