Example email: two sentences on what the startup shipped, one customer result, one question. Stay under 60 words.

<!-- K-422 -->
Example LinkedIn note: one line, founder to founder, no pitch.

<!-- K-423 -->
SMS: under 160 characters, name the rep, include opt-out text. Warm prospects only.
